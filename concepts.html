Lab Prep Checklist

  • Install drivers and verify device detection (e.g., uhd_find_devices).
  • Use appropriate antennas & attenuators; keep TX power within lab guidelines.
  • Bring wired headphones; monitor audio to avoid feedback.
  • Keep RF hygiene: terminate unused ports; label cables.

Tools

  • GNU Radio / SDRangel / MATLAB / Python (NumPy/Scipy)
  • Hardware: RTL-SDR, USRP, LimeSDR (per your lab inventory)
